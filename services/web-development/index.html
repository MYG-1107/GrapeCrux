What this service is about

Build a digital presence that explains your value clearly and supports the way your business works. GrapeCrux can help shape and deliver responsive websites, web applications and customer-facing experiences with an emphasis on usability, maintainability and performance.

What the work may include

Corporate websites

Clear, responsive pages that make your business and services easy to understand.

Web applications

Purpose-built workflows, dashboards and digital tools designed for the people who use them.

E-commerce experiences

Product pages, shopping journeys and integrations scoped to your operational needs.

API integrations

Connect the services and systems your digital product relies on.

What good delivery should cover

  • A page and navigation structure mapped to user goals.
  • Responsive implementation across desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Accessibility, performance and functionality checks before release.
  • Documentation and handover agreed during project planning.

Our approach

We begin with context and constraints, agree the scope, review the proposed direction, then build and validate the agreed deliverables. Specific milestones, fees and timelines are confirmed only after project discovery.

Frequently asked questions

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. A redesign can begin with a review of the current user journey, content, technical constraints and business goals.

Can a website connect to existing business tools?

Potentially. The available APIs, permissions and data requirements need to be reviewed during discovery.

Do you guarantee search rankings?

No. Technical and content improvements can support discoverability, but search positions are not guaranteed.
