Privacy notice

A plain-language summary of how this static website currently handles information. This draft must be reviewed against the company's actual practices before production use.

Publication note: This is a starter template, not legal advice. Confirm applicable privacy law, the responsible legal entity, contact details, analytics, hosting logs, retention and all third-party services before relying on it.

Information you provide

The project enquiry form in this package is client-side only. When you submit it, the website attempts to open your default email application with the details you entered addressed to service@grapecrux.com. The page does not submit that form to a website server or store it in a website database.

Email and third-party handling

If you send the email, your email service and the recipient's email environment will process it according to their own systems and policies. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information in a project brief.

Chat assistant

The included GrapeCrux Assistant uses simple, local rules in your browser. In this package, it does not call a generative AI provider or send chat messages to a chatbot server. If a hosted AI provider, analytics tool, CRM or form service is added later, this notice must be updated to describe the service, purpose, legal basis where applicable, sharing and retention accurately.

Cookies and analytics

This template does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Hosting providers may keep technical logs as part of operating their service. Verify the actual hosting configuration and any services added before publication.

Your choices and contact

For questions about a project enquiry or personal information, contact service@grapecrux.com. Add the company's approved privacy contact and any legally required rights, processes and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before the website is used commercially.

Review before launch

Review this notice whenever form handling, analytics, chat integrations, hosting, storage or other data practices change.
