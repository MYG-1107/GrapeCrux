Website strategy

What to define before starting a company website

A website is a business tool, not just a set of screens. Before design starts, make a short brief that covers the audience, the main decisions visitors need to make, the pages they need, content ownership and what a successful enquiry looks like.

  1. Audience: who needs the information and what do they already know?
  2. Primary action: contact, purchase, book, compare or learn?
  3. Content: who can approve service details, company claims and portfolio evidence?
  4. Constraints: current hosting, domain, integrations, privacy, accessibility and maintenance.
  5. Measurement: decide what to measure before launch and obtain appropriate consent for analytics.
AI & automation

How to scope a useful AI assistant

Choose one clear user task first. Define what information the assistant is allowed to use, what it must not answer, how uncertainty is shown and when a human should take over. Protect provider credentials on a backend, test ordinary and adversarial inputs, and keep a fallback for missing information. A chat interface alone does not make a system a reliable AI product.

Quality assurance

What to test before a website launch

  • Every navigation link and primary call to action.
  • Direct loading of each intended clean URL and legacy redirect where configured.
  • Forms: required fields, invalid values, confirmations and actual delivery.
  • Mobile and tablet layouts, keyboard navigation, focus states and text contrast.
  • Page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt and meaningful alt text.
  • Browser console, failed network requests, image loading and measured performance.

Record results, device and browser details, expected behaviour and reproducible steps for any defect.
