A coherent brand system that helps customers recognise and understand your business.

What this service is about

A strong brand is more than a logo. It is the positioning, language and visual system that helps a business communicate consistently. Work should be grounded in your audience, market and real differentiators.

What the work may include

Brand direction

Clarify audience, positioning, differentiators and desired perception.

Visual identity

Develop a considered visual direction and supporting identity elements.

Brand guidelines

Document how the identity should be used across common touchpoints.

Digital collateral

Prepare approved brand assets for website, presentations and digital communications.

What good delivery should cover

  • A clear brief and agreed creative direction.
  • Consistent typography, colour and visual hierarchy.
  • Practical files and guidance for future use.
  • Handover of agreed asset formats and usage notes.

Our approach

We begin with context and constraints, agree the scope, review the proposed direction, then build and validate the agreed deliverables. Specific milestones, fees and timelines are confirmed only after project discovery.

Frequently asked questions

Can you refresh an existing brand?

Yes. A refresh can retain recognisable brand equity while improving consistency and usability.

Will the process include stakeholder feedback?

Feedback and approval checkpoints should be agreed in the project plan.

Do you provide print production?

The scope can include production-ready design files; physical production should be agreed separately.
