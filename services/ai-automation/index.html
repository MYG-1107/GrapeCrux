What this service is about

Use AI where it creates practical value: helping people find information, reducing repetitive work and connecting knowledge to business processes. Every use case should start with a defined workflow, approved source material and appropriate privacy safeguards.

What the work may include

Service and support assistants

Guided assistants that answer from approved business information and route requests when needed.

Knowledge tools

Search and summarisation experiences for approved documents and internal knowledge.

Workflow automation

Automate defined repetitive tasks where tools, data and review steps are understood.

AI integration

Connect an AI capability to a product or system through a secure backend design.

What good delivery should cover

  • Use-case definition and success criteria.
  • A reviewed source of truth for the assistant or workflow.
  • Clear fallback behaviour when information is missing.
  • Security, privacy and human-review considerations documented.

Our approach

We begin with context and constraints, agree the scope, review the proposed direction, then build and validate the agreed deliverables. Specific milestones, fees and timelines are confirmed only after project discovery.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a chatbot for our website?

A website assistant can be scoped around approved company FAQs, service information and enquiry handoff. A production generative AI version requires a backend and model provider configuration.

Can an AI assistant guarantee correct answers?

No AI system can guarantee every response. Retrieval limits, testing, fallback rules and human escalation are important.

Will the AI system need access to our data?

That depends on the use case. Data access should be minimised and explicitly designed, with permission and retention rules.
