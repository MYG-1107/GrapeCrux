How engagements begin

Understand first. Agree the scope. Then build.

We begin with the current process, systems, users and constraints. Before work starts, both sides should understand the deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, acceptance criteria and communication cadence.

Discovery and requirements

Clarify the problem, actors, integrations, current tools and expected outcomes.

Scope and technical plan

Agree a practical approach, milestones, security responsibilities and assumptions.

Delivery and validation

Share progress, review working increments and test against agreed acceptance criteria.

Handover and support

Document the solution and define any maintenance or future improvement arrangement.

Discuss a requirement

Tell us what you need to build, connect or improve.

Share the business context and the outcome you want. The team can review the fit and suggest a sensible next step.
