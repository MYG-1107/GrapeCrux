Privacy notice

A plain-language summary of how this website currently handles project enquiries, the guided assistant and optional analytics measurement.

Who operates this website: GrapeCrux is a creative initiative of Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited. This notice describes the current static website setup and should be updated whenever the company changes its data practices or adds a third-party service.

Information you provide

The project enquiry form in this package is client-side only. When you submit it, the website attempts to open your default email application with the details you entered addressed to service@grapecrux.com. The page does not submit that form to a website server or store it in a website database.

Email and third-party handling

If you send the email, your email service and the recipient's email environment will process it according to their own systems and policies. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information in a project brief.

Chat assistant

The default GrapeCrux Assistant uses a limited set of local, rule-based answers in the browser and does not send chat messages to a server. A separately configured hosted AI backend may process messages if the company enables it; the notice, provider details and retention practices must be reviewed and updated before that integration is activated.

Google Analytics

This website is configured with Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-QXMRMGJEP2) to understand aggregate website usage, such as page views and interactions. Optional analytics measurement is configured as denied by default. A page-view event is sent only after a visitor selects “Allow analytics” in the privacy preferences. If analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as browser/device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed and interaction events. Google processes this information under its own terms and policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Visitors can reject optional analytics or reopen “Cookie preferences” from the footer. The preference is stored in the browser on that device. Clearing browser storage may reset the choice. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation signals are configured as denied in this website package.

Cookies and hosting

Essential technical storage may be used to remember the analytics preference. The hosting provider may maintain server logs necessary to operate and secure the website. Verify the active hosting, analytics property settings, data-retention period and any additional integrations before publication.

Your choices and contact

For questions about a project enquiry or personal information, contact service@grapecrux.com. Add the company's approved privacy contact and any legally required rights, processes and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before the website is used commercially.

Updates to this notice

This notice should be reviewed whenever form handling, analytics, chat integrations, hosting, storage or other data practices change. The company should also confirm that the stated contact and privacy processes reflect its current operations.
