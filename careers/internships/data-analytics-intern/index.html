About the opportunity

What this role is about

Help turn structured data into clear information. Practise data cleaning, SQL analysis, metric validation and visual communication around business questions.

GrapeCrux values practical problem-solving, clear communication and responsible engineering. The work is shaped around business needs, user outcomes and the quality of the solution delivered.

Before you apply

Use Job ID GC-IN-007 in any follow-up about this opportunity. The application email includes this reference automatically.

GrapeCrux does not charge applicants any application or recruitment fee.

What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Clean, organise and validate datasets from approved sources.
  • Write SQL queries to filter, join and summarise information.
  • Use spreadsheets or Python to inspect trends and anomalies.
  • Create understandable charts, reports or dashboard views.
  • Check metric definitions and explain calculation assumptions.
  • Present findings in concise notes for technical and business audiences.
What you should bring

Required skills and experience

  • Basic spreadsheet skills and comfort with formulas.
  • Introductory SQL or equivalent data-query learning.
  • Basic statistics and ability to interpret averages and trends.
  • Attention to data quality and avoiding unsupported conclusions.
  • Clear communication and ability to explain analytical choices.
Additional strengths

Helpful experience

  • Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio or similar tools.
  • Python with pandas or experience cleaning datasets.
  • A dashboard, spreadsheet analysis or short data report.
How contribution is assessed

What success can look like

  • Analysis is accurate, reproducible and tied to a clear question.
  • Visuals communicate without overstating evidence.
  • Data definitions, filters and assumptions are explained.
