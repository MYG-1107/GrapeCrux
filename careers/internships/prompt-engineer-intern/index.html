About the opportunity

What this role is about

Explore how clear instructions, examples and evaluation improve AI-assisted workflows. Create test prompts, compare outputs and document where human judgement is needed.

GrapeCrux values practical problem-solving, clear communication and responsible engineering. The work is shaped around business needs, user outcomes and the quality of the solution delivered.

Before you apply

Use Job ID GC-IN-002 in any follow-up about this opportunity. The application email includes this reference automatically.

GrapeCrux does not charge applicants any application or recruitment fee.

What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Draft and refine prompts for specific user or business tasks.
  • Create representative test cases and compare outputs against clear criteria.
  • Experiment with examples, constraints, structured formats and instruction hierarchy.
  • Record failure patterns such as unsupported claims or missing context.
  • Help prepare evaluation notes and simple prompt test datasets.
  • Identify where human review or fallback behaviour is needed.
What you should bring

Required skills and experience

  • Clear written communication and ability to express requirements precisely.
  • Attention to detail and willingness to test assumptions.
  • Basic familiarity with AI assistants or generative AI concepts.
  • Analytical thinking and comfort organising examples and results.
  • Ability to explain work and follow data-handling instructions.
Additional strengths

Helpful experience

  • AI tools, language, research or workflow-automation projects.
  • JSON, spreadsheets, Python or simple evaluation scripts.
  • Interest in retrieval, responsible AI or prompt-injection risks.
How contribution is assessed

What success can look like

  • Prompts are tied to a defined task and evaluation criteria.
  • Test notes capture successful outputs and failure cases.
  • Recommendations improve consistency without hiding uncertainty.
