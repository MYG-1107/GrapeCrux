About the opportunity

What this role is about

Protect product quality by testing important user journeys, finding reproducible defects and strengthening release checks. Combine exploratory thinking with structured test design and automation where useful.

GrapeCrux values practical problem-solving, clear communication and responsible engineering. The work is shaped around business needs, user outcomes and the quality of the solution delivered.

What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Review requirements and acceptance criteria to identify risks and test scenarios.
  • Plan and execute functional, exploratory, regression and cross-browser tests.
  • Test APIs, integrations, forms, role permissions and data validation paths.
  • Create and maintain automated checks using appropriate browser or API frameworks.
  • Record defects with reproducible steps, evidence, severity and expected behaviour.
  • Contribute accessibility, performance and release-readiness checks where relevant.
What you should bring

Required skills and experience

  • Experience in software testing or quality assurance for web applications or digital products.
  • Understanding of test planning, cases, defect lifecycle and risk-based prioritisation.
  • Ability to test user journeys, APIs, data flows and integrations.
  • Clear defect reporting and communication with developers.
  • Working knowledge of version control and at least one test or scripting tool.
Additional strengths

Helpful experience

  • Playwright, Selenium, Cypress, Postman or similar tools.
  • Accessibility testing, performance testing or authorised security testing.
  • Regression checks in CI/CD pipelines.
How contribution is assessed

What success can look like

  • Important journeys have clear coverage and release risks are visible early.
  • Defects are reproducible, prioritised and retested.
  • Automation protects stable critical paths without becoming brittle.
