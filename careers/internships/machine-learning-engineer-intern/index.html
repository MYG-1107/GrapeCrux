About the opportunity

What this role is about

Learn the machine-learning workflow: preparing data, establishing baselines, evaluating models and explaining results with appropriate caution.

GrapeCrux values practical problem-solving, clear communication and responsible engineering. The work is shaped around business needs, user outcomes and the quality of the solution delivered.

Before you apply

Use Job ID GC-IN-006 in any follow-up about this opportunity. The application email includes this reference automatically.

GrapeCrux does not charge applicants any application or recruitment fee.

What you'll do

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and inspect datasets for completeness, consistency and leakage.
  • Perform exploratory analysis and document observations.
  • Build or adapt baseline models with established Python libraries.
  • Evaluate models with suitable metrics and validation approaches.
  • Compare experiments and record assumptions and limitations.
  • Present findings in notebooks, charts or short written summaries.
What you should bring

Required skills and experience

  • Basic Python programming and comfort working with data.
  • Introductory statistics, probability or quantitative reasoning.
  • Understanding of training, validation and overfitting.
  • Ability to interpret simple charts and metrics accurately.
  • Careful documentation and willingness to question unexpected results.
Additional strengths

Helpful experience

  • Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn or Jupyter notebooks.
  • Classification, regression, clustering or feature engineering.
  • SQL or dataset preparation experience.
How contribution is assessed

What success can look like

  • Experiments are reproducible and use appropriate validation.
  • Results are interpreted in context, including uncertainty.
  • Data and model assumptions are documented.
