Privacy notice

A plain-language summary of how this website currently handles project enquiries, the guided assistant and optional analytics measurement.

Who operates this website: GrapeCrux is a creative initiative of Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited. This notice describes the current static website setup and should be updated whenever the company changes its data practices or adds a third-party service.

Project enquiry information

The contact form requests your name, business email, country, requested service and project description. Company name, phone number, project type, budget range, expected start date, existing website and preferred contact method help the team understand the requirement. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government IDs, patient data or other sensitive information.

When the server-side enquiry service is configured, the website sends the form to the GrapeCrux API. The API validates the information, creates an enquiry reference and stores the record in a private database on the separately hosted backend. The record may also contain the page where the enquiry was started, the referring page and campaign parameters such as UTM source and campaign. A hashed network identifier is used for operational abuse controls; the raw IP address is not intentionally stored in the enquiry record.

The company notification email and customer acknowledgement are sent through the configured email provider. Email delivery is separate from database storage; a successful on-screen receipt confirms that the enquiry was stored, not that every email provider delivered a message.

If the server-side service is not configured or cannot confirm storage, the website provides a clearly labelled email option addressed to service@grapecrux.com. In that fallback flow, the visitor must review and send the email manually; no website database receipt is created.

Lead records and retention

Enquiry records are intended for reviewing and responding to project requests, managing follow-up and maintaining a record of business discussions. The backend includes a configurable retention setting with a default of 365 days and a manual backup and deletion utility. The company must approve the final retention period, configure it on the backend host and perform deletion under its approved policy. Database backups must be stored separately with restricted access and the same retention controls.

Lead records are accessible through an authenticated internal dashboard. Access should be limited to authorised team members. The backend must be deployed with a private persistent storage volume, HTTPS, strong secrets and suitable access controls. The website repository does not contain production credentials.

Email and third-party handling

If you email service@grapecrux.com directly, your email provider and the recipient's email environment will process the message according to their own systems and policies. If SMTP notifications or acknowledgement messages are enabled for the enquiry service, the configured mail provider will process the relevant message data. Do not include passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive information in a project brief.

Chat assistant

The default GrapeCrux Assistant uses a curated set of local, rule-based answers in the browser. If a hosted AI assistant backend is configured, visitor messages are sent to that backend and may be processed by the AI provider selected by the company. The provider, purpose and applicable retention settings must be confirmed and this notice updated before enabling that service for production visitors. Do not send sensitive personal information through the assistant.

Google Analytics

This website is configured with Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-QXMRMGJEP2) to understand aggregate website usage, such as page views, service discovery, enquiry form starts, CTA interactions and confirmed enquiry receipts when the online form is enabled. Optional analytics measurement is configured as denied by default. Analytics events are sent only after a visitor allows analytics in the cookie preferences. Event parameters are designed to exclude names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names and free-text project descriptions. A generate_lead event is sent only after the backend confirms that an enquiry record has been stored; a contact-page visit or button click is not treated as a lead. If analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process information such as browser/device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed and non-identifying interaction events. Google processes this information under its own terms and policies. Review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Visitors can reject optional analytics or reopen “Cookie preferences” from the footer. The preference is stored in the browser on that device. Clearing browser storage may reset the choice. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation signals are configured as denied in this website package.

Cookies, session storage and hosting

Essential technical storage may be used to remember the analytics preference. The website may use session storage to remember non-identifying landing-page and campaign attribution for the duration of a browser tab/session so an enquiry can include its source. The hosting provider may maintain server logs necessary to operate and secure the website. The enquiry database is hosted separately from GitHub Pages; its host, storage region, backup location and access arrangements must be documented by the company before production use. Review the active hosting, analytics property settings, data-retention period and any additional integrations regularly.

Your choices and contact

You may contact service@grapecrux.com with questions about a project enquiry or personal information, including a request to correct or delete an enquiry record where applicable. The company should verify the requester before changing or disclosing a record. The organisation must confirm its applicable legal bases, data-subject processes, privacy contact, hosting region and jurisdiction-specific notices before using the backend for production enquiries.

Updates to this notice

This notice should be reviewed whenever form handling, analytics, chat integrations, hosting, storage or other data practices change. The company should also confirm that the stated contact and privacy processes reflect its current operations.
