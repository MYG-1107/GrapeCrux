## 1. Executive assessment

GrapeCrux is presented as a creative initiative of **Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited**, not a separate incorporated company. The About page states that the legal entity was incorporated on 27 November 2019 in Pune, Maharashtra, and displays the company CIN and registered-office details supplied by the company.

From a prospective client's perspective, the website now explains the brand's purpose, service catalogue, delivery approach, possible industry use cases and enquiry path more clearly. The strongest content improvement is the distinction between actual public evidence and illustrative material: the Work page labels its examples as concepts, and the website does not invent client names, testimonials, credentials or project results.

The current package has **28 canonical, indexable routes**, including eight service pages, an Industries page, six new blog articles and Careers. Static validation reports no broken local targets, missing page metadata or duplicate canonical URLs. The deterministic chatbot knowledge tests pass 23/23.

## 3. Service catalogue review

1. **Web & App Development** — corporate websites, portals, web applications, e-commerce experiences and integrations.

3. **Product Design & UX** — discovery, journeys, wireframes, prototypes, interface systems and handoff.
4. **Brand Strategy & Visual Identity** — positioning, visual direction, logo systems, guidelines and digital collateral.
5. **Digital Growth & SEO** — technical SEO, content planning, analytics and conversion improvement.
6. **Cloud & DevOps** — deployment automation, CI/CD, monitoring, infrastructure reviews and reliability.
7. **Data & System Integration** — APIs, data processing, validation, database features and dashboards.
8. **Quality Assurance & Technical Consulting** — functional, responsive, accessibility and performance checks, plus release readiness.

**QA judgement:** These categories are valid as a service menu, but a website cannot independently prove the company's real staffing, certifications or execution capacity. The company should confirm which services it is ready to contract and deliver before promoting them as active offerings.

## 4. Portfolio, proof and credibility

The Work page now makes it explicit that its three examples—corporate digital presence, service-discovery assistant and operations dashboard—are concepts, not completed client work. It also states that the site does not currently publish verified client case studies or testimonials.

This honesty is correct, but it remains the largest sales-trust gap. Prospective clients normally look for proof such as:

- the client's challenge and context;
- the scope and deliverables actually completed;
- technology or methods used, where relevant;
- measured outcomes with a clear measurement period;
- an approved quote or reference, where permission exists.

Do not add generic client logos, made-up statistics, fabricated reviews or imply that a concept is an engagement that has already been delivered.

## 6. Careers and internship content added

The Careers page describes seven remote, work-from-home internship tracks, each with role purpose and basic expectations:

1. Software Development Intern (frontend, backend or full-stack)
2. AI / Machine Learning Intern
3. Data Analytics Intern
4. Quality Assurance & Test Automation Intern
5. UI / UX Design Intern
6. Cloud & DevOps Intern
7. Business & Digital Marketing Intern

The page describes a three-month programme, an indicative commitment of around 20 hours per week and an indicative stipend range of ₹5,000–₹10,000 per month. These are **proposed website terms**, not a verified company-approved offer. The written offer must confirm the stipend, payment schedule, start/end dates, weekly hours, mentor, role scope and selection details. The page also states that internship completion does not guarantee a permanent job and that applicants should never pay recruitment or security fees.

Role-card Apply buttons now select the matching role in the application form and move focus to the form. The form prepares an email; it does not store applications or upload CVs. Applicants must attach their resume to the email that opens.

**Before publishing active vacancies:** confirm available seats, actual mentor capacity, stipend budget and start dates internally. Do not add JobPosting structured data until each opening has accurate, approved vacancy details and a real application path.

## 7. Chatbot QA

The assistant is labelled as a guided FAQ assistant, not a human representative or a live generative AI model. Its curated responses cover:

- GrapeCrux identity and its relationship to Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited;
- stated Pune registered-office location and supplied CIN/incorporation information;
- all eight published service areas;
- illustrative industry contexts;
- concept-only portfolio and the absence of published verified testimonials;
- project process, pricing and timeline limitations;
- SEO ranking guarantees (it explicitly does not promise first place);
- AI/automation scope and the assistant's own rule-based nature;
- blog topics and the internship programme;
- application, contact and privacy handling.

The message renderer uses text output for user messages rather than injecting the visitor's text as HTML. The assistant does not collect sensitive data. With the current empty frontend API configuration, responses are local and deterministic. The optional FastAPI backend is separate and is not considered live until hosted and securely configured.

**Automated test result:** 23/23 chatbot knowledge intent tests passed, including identity, location, services, industry context, testimonials, project proof, ranking guarantees, pricing, AI scope, internship details, CV/application handling, contact, certifications and a prompt asking the assistant to fabricate testimonials.

## 9. Google Analytics and privacy

GA4 measurement ID: **G-QXMRMGJEP2**.

The GA4 loader and configuration appear once in the `<head>` of each HTML document in the package. The tag defaults analytics and advertising storage to denied. The site sends a page-view event only after the visitor selects **Allow analytics**. Reject keeps analytics denied; the footer's Cookie preferences control reopens the choice panel. The Privacy page describes the intended behaviour.

This is a source-level implementation check. After deployment, validate the actual consent sequence with Google Tag Assistant and GA4 Realtime. Review the privacy/consent setup against the company's target markets and legal requirements.

## 12. What could not be verified live

The live domain did not return content to the available browser/fetch environment during this run, and outbound DNS/network access from the local test container was unavailable. A full Chromium session could not be completed. Therefore this report does **not** claim that live mobile interactions, actual mail-client handoff, live GA4 Realtime events, PageSpeed/Lighthouse scores or a hosted AI backend were physically observed.

After upload, manually verify:

1. Open the homepage, About, Services and all eight service detail pages, Industries, Work, Blogs, all six articles, Careers, Contact, Privacy and Terms directly.
2. Check the shared logo, header and footer on desktop, tablet and mobile. Test the mobile menu, active section state and Escape behaviour.
3. Open the chatbot and try the 23 test prompts from `tests/chatbot-intents.test.cjs`; verify unknown questions do not invent facts.
4. On Careers, click every Apply for this track button and confirm the matching role is selected. Submit a safe test application only with approval and ensure the email draft is correct before sending.
5. Test the Contact form with a dummy test brief and verify the correct service, budget, currency and message appear in the email draft. Do not send personal test data to the real mailbox without permission.
6. In a fresh browser, verify the analytics preference panel. Reject optional analytics and confirm no page-view event is sent; then test Allow analytics and check one page view in Tag Assistant/GA4 Realtime. Confirm footer preferences reopen the panel.
7. Test old `.html` URLs, clean route redirects, 404 page, browser back/forward, and direct refresh on nested routes.
8. Run Lighthouse/PageSpeed for homepage, service detail, blog article and contact. Test keyboard access, contrast, focus visibility, labels, reduced motion and 320px mobile width.
9. Submit `https://grapecrux.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and monitor indexing/canonical reports.
10. Confirm internship openings, stipend, seat capacity, mentor and dates with the company before treating the page as an active recruitment commitment.
