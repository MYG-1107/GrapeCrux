Before you contact us

What happens after you prepare an enquiry?

The first message helps us understand whether the requirement matches the services described on this site. It is not a commitment to purchase.

01

Share the context

Tell us about the organisation, users, current systems, business problem and the result you want.

02

Clarify the requirement

Where there appears to be a fit, the next discussion can clarify scope, technical dependencies, constraints and open questions.

03

Review a written proposal

Before work starts, review the deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, milestones, fees and payment terms.

Questions clients often ask

Can I enquire before my requirements are final?

Yes. Describe the problem and what you know so far. We can identify the information needed to scope the next step.

How is project pricing decided?

Pricing depends on deliverables, complexity, integrations, risks, dependencies and the agreed delivery approach. The proposal should identify the pricing basis and relevant third-party costs.

Can you work with software we already use?

Potentially. The team needs to review the platform, access model, API or export capabilities, documentation and vendor restrictions before confirming feasibility.

Do you work on healthcare or financial software?

We can discuss software engineering, integration, data handling and security requirements in these areas. Clinical, medical, legal, regulatory and financial decisions must remain with appropriately qualified professionals. Requirements and responsibilities must be assessed before any regulated work is accepted.

Does this website store my enquiry?
