Frequently asked questions

Before you apply.

Is this a paid internship?

No. This is an unpaid internship. GrapeCrux does not provide a salary or stipend for participation.

Do students need to pay anything?

No. There is no application fee, registration fee, training fee, deposit or participation fee. Do not pay anyone to apply.

Which roles can I apply for?

Software Developer Intern, Data Analytics Intern, AI Intern, Forward Deployment Intern and QA Testing Intern. Select your preferred role from the application dropdown.

Can I apply if I have only academic projects?

Yes. You can describe coursework, lab work, academic projects, personal projects or independent learning. Explain your own contribution honestly.

Is a resume compulsory?

No. A resume is optional. You can still submit the form with your skills and project details.

Does applying guarantee selection or a job?

No. Applications are reviewed and shortlisted applicants may be contacted for further discussion. This internship application is not a job offer or placement guarantee.
