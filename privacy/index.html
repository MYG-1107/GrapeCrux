Privacy notice

How GrapeCrux handles project enquiries, job applications, the guided assistant and optional analytics.

Website operator: GrapeCrux is a creative initiative of Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited. This notice describes the current static website and its hosted form-delivery provider.

Project enquiry information

When you submit the Contact form, the details you provide are sent over HTTPS to FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards the message to service@grapecrux.com. The form may include your name, business email, company, country, phone, requested service, project type, indicative budget, expected start date, existing website, preferred contact method, project description and non-identifying campaign attribution. The GrapeCrux website does not operate a separate enquiry database or private lead-management dashboard.

FormSubmit may make submissions available in its provider-side archive for up to 30 days. The provider's current handling, technical limits and terms are described in its official documentation. After delivery, the message is also handled by the recipient's email provider and mailbox. Email retention and access are managed by the organisation's email account and policies.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, patient records or other sensitive or regulated information through the website. The Contact form includes a required privacy acknowledgement before submission.

Careers applications and CV attachments

When you submit a Careers application, the form-delivery provider forwards your application details to yaswanth@grapecrux.com. Details may include your contact information, selected role and Job ID, education, experience, role-specific answers and professional profile links. You may optionally attach one resume or CV in PDF, DOC or DOCX format. The total upload must not exceed 10 MB.

Careers forms include a required privacy acknowledgement. The website does not host applicant records or store uploaded CVs in its own database. The provider processes the form and attachment for delivery, and the recipient mailbox is then subject to the organisation's access and retention practices. Please do not attach identity documents or include banking credentials, passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Hosted form provider

FormSubmit is an independent third-party service used to deliver website form submissions by email without a custom application backend. On the first submission to each recipient address, the provider may send an activation email to that recipient. The recipient must complete the provider's one-time activation step before normal form delivery is enabled. Please review the provider's current documentation and terms for details of its processing and retention.

Guided assistant and project assessment

The GrapeCrux assistant uses a curated set of local, rule-based answers in your browser. The Project Assessment tool also calculates suggested service routes in the browser. These tools do not send your answers to an AI service or a GrapeCrux application server. If you choose to continue to Contact, the information you enter in that separate form is processed as described above.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-QXMRMGJEP2) to understand aggregate website usage, including page views, service discovery, form starts and website interactions. Optional analytics is denied by default and is sent only after you allow analytics in the cookie preferences. Events are designed not to include names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names, CV contents or free-text answers.

The form_submit event records a form-submission attempt in the browser. It does not prove that FormSubmit delivered an email or that a recipient read it. The website does not emit a generate_lead event as a verified email-delivery conversion. Google may process browser, device, approximate network location, page and interaction data under its own terms. Review Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reject optional analytics or reopen “Cookie preferences” from the footer. The preference is stored in your browser. Clearing browser storage may reset your choice. Advertising storage and personalisation signals are configured as denied.

Cookies, hosting and retention

Essential browser storage may be used to remember your analytics preference. Session storage may retain non-identifying page and campaign attribution for the current browser session. The static website is hosted separately from the form-delivery provider. GrapeCrux does not store enquiry or application submissions in a website-side database. Messages delivered to company mailboxes are subject to the organisation's email access and retention policy; users should contact the company to ask about a specific enquiry or application.

Your choices and contact

For questions about a project enquiry, application or personal information, contact service@grapecrux.com. The organisation may need to verify your request before disclosing, correcting or deleting a message. The company should review its applicable legal obligations, mailbox access, provider terms and retention practices regularly.

Updates to this notice

This notice should be updated whenever form providers, recipient addresses, analytics, hosting or data-handling practices change.
