How we approach the work

Each project is scoped around its users, systems, dependencies and acceptance criteria. The steps below describe a typical working pattern; the final plan is agreed with the client.

01

Decide what task AI should support and what it must not decide.

02

Review data sources, permissions, confidentiality, retention and provider requirements.

03

Select an approach such as retrieval, structured extraction or a workflow tool based on fit.

04

Test representative, adversarial and failure scenarios before release.

05

Agree monitoring, escalation, evaluation and update responsibilities.

Security, quality and ownership

Access should follow least-privilege principles. Data flows, error handling, testing responsibilities, documentation, handover and post-launch ownership should be agreed before delivery. Regulated or high-impact use cases need an additional review of applicable obligations and qualified oversight.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI assistant use our internal documents?

Potentially. Sources, access rights, document quality, retention and retrieval behaviour must be assessed. Access should follow the user's actual permissions.

Can AI make decisions without human review?

Some low-risk tasks may be automated with controls. High-impact or uncertain outputs should have a defined human review and escalation path.

Can you guarantee every AI answer is correct?

No. AI systems can produce errors. Retrieval grounding, evaluation, constrained outputs, clear uncertainty and human oversight reduce risk but do not eliminate it.
