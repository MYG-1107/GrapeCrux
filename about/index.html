Who we are

An entrepreneurial initiative built around possibility.

GrapeCrux is a brand and creative initiative of Infinite Resource Creators Private Limited, an Indian private limited company incorporated on 27 November 2019 in Pune, Maharashtra.

Supported by the entrepreneurial vision of its parent company, GrapeCrux explores digital innovation, creative services, technology-enabled solutions and emerging business opportunities for individuals, entrepreneurs, startups and businesses.

We believe every idea holds potential. With imagination, resources, collaboration and purposeful execution, that potential can become a meaningful outcome. Our commitment is to encourage innovation, embrace new possibilities and develop solutions for an evolving digital and interconnected world.
