How we work

A transparent route from the first conversation to handover.

A good engagement makes the work understandable before it begins. We clarify the problem, identify dependencies and agree what will be delivered, how it will be reviewed and what happens after release.

The delivery path

Clear stages. Shared decisions.

The exact sequence adapts to the size and risk of the project, but the decisions should remain visible to everyone involved.

01

Initial consultation

Understand the business goal, users, current process, systems, constraints and the reason the work matters.

02

Requirements and feasibility

Clarify user journeys, integrations, data access, technical dependencies, risks and open questions.

03

Estimate and proposal

Document deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, pricing basis, third-party costs, milestones and responsibilities.

04

Agreement and planning

Confirm decision-makers, communication cadence, access, approvals, acceptance criteria and change control.

05

Build and review

Deliver in reviewable increments, discuss questions early and record agreed changes to scope.

06

Test and validate

Check the agreed journeys, integrations, responsive layouts, accessibility and relevant security controls.

07

Deploy and hand over

Coordinate release responsibilities, documentation, environment details and source/asset handover as agreed.

08

Support and improve

Define any ongoing monitoring, maintenance, incident response or future enhancement arrangement separately.

Scope clarity

What a proposal should explain.

Included deliverables

Pages, features, workflows, interfaces, integrations, tests, documentation and handover outputs.

Exclusions and assumptions

What is outside scope, which third parties are involved and which assumptions affect the estimate.

Client responsibilities

Content, access, subject-matter input, approvals, test users, data quality and timely decisions.

Pricing and payments

Fixed-scope or time-based pricing, payment milestones, licences, hosting, usage and change requests.

Acceptance and change

How work is reviewed, what counts as acceptance and how additional requirements are evaluated.

Support and ownership

Who operates the solution after launch, how issues are handled and what maintenance is covered.

No universal price or schedule: fees and delivery plans are prepared after the actual scope, complexity, integrations and dependencies are reviewed. No fixed response time or outcome is promised on this page.
