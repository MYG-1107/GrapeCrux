Life at GrapeCrux

Good work comes from good ways of working.

We aim to create an environment where people understand the problem, communicate clearly and take responsibility for the quality of what they build.

01

Start with the problem

Understand the user, business context and expected outcome before choosing a technology or building a feature.

02

Share progress early

Ask focused questions, communicate blockers and document decisions so teammates can contribute with context.

03

Build with care

Think about testing, privacy, accessibility, reliability and maintainability—not only whether a demo works.

04

Learn through feedback

Use reviews and reflection to strengthen technical judgement, delivery habits and collaboration.

Careers questions

Before you apply.

Which full-time positions are listed?

Senior Full-stack Developer, AI Engineer, Forward Deployment Engineer, Quality Assurance Tester, DevOps Engineer and Big Data Engineer.

Which internship tracks are available?

Software Developer, Prompt Engineer, AI Engineer, Python Developer, .NET Developer, Machine Learning Engineer and Data Analytics.

Is there a fixed end date for full-time roles?

Full-time positions are ongoing opportunities with no pre-set end date. Applicable employment conditions are discussed during recruitment and provided in the written terms.

How do I submit my application?

Choose a role, complete the relevant fields and submit the form. Your application details are sent to yaswanth@grapecrux.com through our hosted form-delivery provider. You may attach a PDF, DOC or DOCX resume (maximum 10 MB).

Why are the questions different for internships and full-time positions?

Full-time applicants are asked about employment history, experience and responsibilities. Internship applicants are asked about education, current status and academic or personal projects.

Can I apply without a long professional work history?

Yes. Internship applicants can describe coursework, academic projects, personal builds or independent learning. Be clear about your individual contribution.
